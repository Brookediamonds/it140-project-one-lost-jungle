## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

The Lost Jungle is an adventure game about exploring a mysterious jungle, collecting important items, and surviving dangeous obstacles. The game combines exploration, problem-solving, and adventure as the player searches for an ancient treasure.

**Storyline:**

The player becomes stranded in a mysterious jungle while searching for a legendary treasure. To survive and reach the treasure, the player must explore the different rooms and collect six important items: a compass, machete, ancient map, first aid kit, antidote, and a golden key. Each item will help the player along the journey. The player must collect all six items before entering the Villain's Lair, where the Jungle Beast, also known as the Ancient Jungle Guardian, is waiting. If the player enters without collecting everything, the guardian will win. If all six items are collected, the player can defeat the guardian and escape the jungle.

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Compass
2. Machete
3. Ancient Map
4. First Aid Kit
5. Antidote
6. Golden Key

If you add rooms beyond the minimum, add an item for every additional room
except the start room and villain room.

## Villain

The villain is the Jungle Beast, also known as the Ancient Jungle Guardian. It protacts the ancient treasure and tries to stop anyone from escaping the jungle. The player must collect all six items before facing the guardian.
